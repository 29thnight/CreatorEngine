CreatorEngine reformats the three 32x32 planes into RGB-style (a,b,albedo)
triples. The coefficients themselves are unchanged. SheenLtc.slang and
sheen-ltc.csv are derived data covered by LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt here.

The numeric material golden is independently generated by CreatorEngine's
CPU reference evaluator. It is not a Blender render or an upstream table.
